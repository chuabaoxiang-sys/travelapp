旅记 · TRIPNOTES

隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

旅记是一个给家人朋友一起记行程、记账的小工具。这份文档说明它收集什么信息、怎么用、跟谁共享。

TripNotes is a small shared trip-planning and expense-tracking tool for families and friends. This page explains what it collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

我们收集什么 · What we collect

  • 登录信息：你的邮箱地址；如果用 Google 登录，还会拿到 Google 账号提供的邮箱和基本资料（姓名、头像）。
    Login info: your email address; if you sign in with Google, also the email and basic profile (name, avatar) Google provides.
  • 你主动填写的内容：行程安排、账目记录、想去的地点、汇率记录、对行程/花费的满意度评价，以及你给自己起的显示名字。
    Content you enter: itinerary items, expense records, wishlist places, exchange rates you log, satisfaction ratings, and your chosen display name.
  • 操作记录：谁在什么时候改动了哪张表的哪条记录（新增/修改/删除），用于团队内部排查问题，不记录具体改了什么内容。
    Audit trail: who changed which record in which table and when (insert/update/delete) — for troubleshooting only, not the content of the change.

怎么用 · How it's used

只用来让你和你邀请的家人朋友一起看到、编辑同一份行程和账目。我们不做广告投放，不把数据卖给第三方，也不用来做跟这个APP无关的分析。

Solely to let you and the people you invite share and edit the same trip and expenses. We don't run ads, sell data to third parties, or use it for anything outside this app.

存在哪里、谁能看到 · Where it's stored, who can see it

数据存在 Supabase（一个第三方数据库服务）里，按"团队"隔离——只有同一个团队（你邀请的人）能看到彼此的行程和账目，看不到其他团队的任何数据。Google 登录本身只用来确认"这是你"，不会把你的 Google 密码或其他 Google 账号数据传给我们。

Data is stored in Supabase (a third-party database service), isolated per "household" — only people you've invited into your household can see each other's trips and expenses; no household can see another's data. Google Sign-In only confirms your identity — we never receive your Google password or other Google account data.

你的选择 · Your choices

你可以随时要求删除你的账号和数据，或者不再使用 Google 登录改用邮箱验证码登录。联系方式见下方。

You can ask to delete your account and data at any time, or stop using Google Sign-In and switch to email code login instead. Contact details below.
